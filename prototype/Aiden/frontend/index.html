One photo. Every finding, what it costs, where to go.

Senyum screens a photo of your teeth and gums for five common conditions at once, explains each in plain language, estimates what you'd pay after CHAS and MediSave, and finds a clinic near you.

A screening and education aid. It does not diagnose and never recommends medication.

    How reliable is it?

    Model test results appear here once the trained model's metrics.json is present.

    • The model was trained on clinical close-up photos from outside Singapore. Phone selfies in home lighting are harder for it.
    • It screens for five conditions only. Gum disease, the most common problem in Singapore adults, is not one of them, so "nothing flagged" never means your mouth is healthy.
    • A photo can't show decay between teeth, problems under the gumline, or how deep anything goes. Only a dentist can.

    Privacy

    Your photo is sent to the screening service, analysed in memory and discarded. It is not saved, logged or used for training. Your residency, card and age answers never leave this browser tab except to compute the estimate, and are not stored. Close the tab and everything is gone. This follows the consent and purpose-limitation obligations of Singapore's PDPA.

    Senyum is a student prototype for IT2226 (Nanyang Polytechnic). Clinic listings are samples. Cost figures are non-binding estimates from the sources cited next to them.
