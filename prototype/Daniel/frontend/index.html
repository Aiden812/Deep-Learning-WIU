For a clearer result

  1. Good light. Face a window or turn on your phone's torch.
  2. Get close. Fill the frame with the teeth or spot you're worried about.
  3. Hold steady. Tap the screen to focus before you take it.
  4. One area at a time. Take another photo for a different spot.

Gigi screens for tartar, decay, missing teeth, mouth ulcers and discolouration. It can't check for gum disease.

        How reliable is Gigi?

        Gigi's screening model is a deep-learning image classifier trained on 4,375 public oral photos in six categories. Here's how it did on photos it had never seen. We show the weak spots too.

        Out of every 100 test photos showing each condition, how many Gigi picked up
        ConditionPicked upBar
        • Not from Singapore. The training photos are not of Singapore patients, so accuracy may differ for you.
        • No gum disease. The most common problem in Singapore adults isn't a category Gigi can screen.
        • One label per photo. A mouth can have more than one condition. Gigi only reports the strongest match.
        • Photo quality matters. Blurry or low-resolution photos lower accuracy, so Gigi asks for a retake when it can tell.

        Your privacy

        • Consent first. Nothing is screened until you tick the consent box.
        • Nothing kept. Your photo is screened in memory and discarded. It's never saved, logged or used to train the model.
        • Location only when asked. "Use my location" is optional, and it's only used to sort clinics by distance.
        • Gone when you leave. Your answers live in this browser tab and disappear when you close it. Handled in line with Singapore's PDPA.
