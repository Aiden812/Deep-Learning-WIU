              Your data (PDPA)

              Photos and videos are checked by a screening server on this computer and kept only for that check. Videos are briefly saved to a temporary file to read their frames, then deleted. Nothing is uploaded, sold or used for training.

              Fair and honest

              The model learned from public photo datasets, not Singapore patients, so it can be wrong, especially in poor light. It can't see gum disease. We never suggest medicine.
