HOW TO READ THIS
- Daniel's full test is the number to compare with groupmates: everyone trains and tests on the same split.
- It contains copies of training photos (about 90% of its images with a condition, see find_leaks.py), so it
  overstates how well any model does on new photos. The clean test removes them; report both.
- The clean test is small for some classes (Calculus 6 images).
- best.pt is the epoch with the highest mAP50-95 on the clean val set (copies of training photos removed).
