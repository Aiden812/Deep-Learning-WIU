STAGE-1 GATE (HEALTHY vs HAS AN ISSUE): MODEL COMPARISON
Aiden's binary classifier (IT2226). Photos it flags go on to the YOLO detector, which finds which condition and where.
Generated by models/classifier/scripts/Aiden/report.py; rewritten every time a model is retrained. Do not edit by hand.
Graphs: figures/comparison_*.png
====================================================================================================

1. SUMMARY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AUROC on the clean test: DenseNet-121 0.9953, DINOv2 ViT-B/14 0.9925. The 95% intervals overlap, so this gap alone is not conclusive.
At thresholds set to catch 95% of clean-val issues: DenseNet-121 catches 94.9% of test issues and clears 98.9% of healthy mouths; DINOv2 ViT-B/14 94.4% and 99.1%.
Issues missed by the gate (never reach YOLO): DenseNet-121 9, DINOv2 ViT-B/14 10 of 178.

2. METHOD (identical for every model; only the backbone and its learning rate differ)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pipeline  Stage 1 (this model): has an issue, or healthy? Flagged photos go to stage 2, YOLO (which condition, where).
Data      Daniel's oral_dataset (data/raw/oral_dataset), target has_issue. Sources: roboflow_oral, zenodo, dentalai,
          kaggle_oral, mendeley_noncaries, screened web images; labelled by people or Qwen (labels.csv: label_by).
Split     Daniel's split. Train: 31279 images, used as is. Val/test: only images with no copy of a training
          photo (find_leaks.py: SIFT keypoint matching on roboflow_oral). About 90% of the val/test images with an
          issue were copies. Clean val: 1135 images. Clean test: 1141 of 3410, used once at the end.
Training  One sigmoid output. Fixed number of epochs (no epoch picked on validation), AdamW wd 0.05, one-cycle cosine,
          mixed precision, BCE with pos_weight + label smoothing 0.05, each photo group equally likely per epoch.
          Augmentation: random resized crop 0.5-1.0, flip, rotation 20, brightness/contrast 0.2 (no hue: discolouration is a colour).
Decision  Probability calibrated on the clean val set. Threshold: the highest that still catches 95% of clean-val issues,
          because a missed issue never reaches YOLO while a false alarm only costs one YOLO run. Test predictions
          average the image and its mirror.

Settings                                            DenseNet-121       DINOv2 ViT-B/14
  image size                                                 224                   224
  epochs                                                      10                    10
  backbone learning rate                                  0.0001                 2e-05
  threshold                                                0.595                 0.874

5. LIMITATIONS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The clean test is small for some conditions (Calculus 6 images), because copies of training photos fill Daniel's val/test.
- Most healthy val/test photos were judged healthy by Qwen, not a person, so a few may hide a small finding.
- Each model was trained once with one learning rate; no other settings were tuned for this comparison.
- Confidence intervals resample images and ignore that some are related, so they are somewhat optimistic.
- The training photos are not from Singapore patients.
